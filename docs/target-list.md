## How to use this list

Per [procurement.md](procurement.md), the person you need is whoever owns **research or field
marketing budget** — not University Recruiting, who cannot fund this. The contacts above are
champions and routers. The two questions to ask each of them:

1. *"Who typically needs to approve investments at this level in your organization?"*
2. *"Has this type of purchase gone through your CFO in the past?"*

And the champion test: ask them to introduce you to that person. Willingness and quality of the
introduction is the measure of whether you have a champion or a fan.
