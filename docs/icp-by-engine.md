# ICP by Engine

There is **no single ICP.** Each engine has its own, and the same company can be a fit for different
engines through different budgets. Derived from the archetypes + the engine fit gates, grounded in
repo evidence ([buyer-needs.md](buyer-needs.md), [who-pays-for-research.md](who-pays-for-research.md),
[question-catalog.md](question-catalog.md)). WTP is UNKNOWN for all until quoted.

| Engine | Best-fit organization | Trigger | Budget owner | Sales hook | Worst fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Research** | developer-facing company whose *deciding* behavior is invisible to its telemetry, with a live product/pricing/onboarding decision | new launch, competitor pressure, onboarding overhaul | Product/UX Research, Growth, PMM | "why the people who didn't choose you didn't" | mature product, median-user questions, well-instrumented |
| **R&D** | org with a **high-uncertainty, parallelizable, prototypeable, evaluable** technical problem | a hard open problem, a capability gap | R&D, Innovation, a product team | "20 independent attempts + the failure map, in a weekend" | low-uncertainty or domain/equipment-heavy problems |
| **Product-Dev** | product team exploring a **solution space / feature demand** | pre-roadmap, new API surface | Product, DX Eng | "the distribution of how builders actually use it" | needs one polished concept, not variety |
| **Activation** | dev-facing company running **credits / startup program / free tier**, needing behavioral adoption evidence | new startup program, credit-budget review | DevRel, Growth, Startup Programs | "do your credits create retention or subsidize activation?" | no adoption motion; typical-user focus |
| **Innovation** | company with a **specific prototypeable problem** it wants options on | a defined challenge, a strategic bet | Innovation, R&D, product | "ranked solutions to *your* problem" | proprietary-context or long-cycle problems |
| **Design-Partner** | product wanting **qualified early design partners** | GA push, ICP refinement | Product, Partnerships | "warm intros to builders who built on you and opted in" | needs volume leads, not fit |
| **Sponsorship** | any brand wanting exposure/recruiting (commodity floor) | recruiting season | Marketing, Recruiting | (do not lead with this) | — |

**The strongest ICP overall** (Research + Activation converging, per [question-catalog.md](question-catalog.md)):

> A developer-facing company spending real money to acquire early-stage builders — credits, startup
> program, free tier — making an allocation or product decision about that spend, where the deciding
> behavior happens **before or outside its own telemetry**.

That single ICP is servable by **two engines at once** (Research: "why don't they choose us / reach
production"; Activation: "do our credits work"), through **two budgets** (Product Research and Startup
Programs) — which is what makes multi-client economics on one cohort real.
