[asset-monetization.md](asset-monetization.md)) via the [company matcher](company-opportunity-map.md)

a web-research pass the current environment can't fully run). **Every WTP is UNKNOWN.** Each row is a
hypothesis to test, and the matcher can still return NO FIT on closer inspection.

## Top 10 to contact first (tailored theses, not "sponsor our hackathon")

1. **A startup-credit program owner** — *"You spend heavily on startup credits. Your telemetry shows
   who redeemed them, not whether they'd have used you anyway, or whether they retain after credits
   end. We're assembling elite builders who choose infrastructure naturally while shipping; varying
   credit exposure and following up at 90 days would tell you which of that spend creates retention.
   Open to a paid pilot?"*
2. **Stripe (Growth)** — *"You publish that 20% of Atlas startups charge within 30 days. You don't
   publish why the other 80% don't. We can observe the full sandbox→production attempt. Pilot?"*
3. **Sentry** — *"Your own posting says founders don't read whitepapers and you need to know what they
   do read. We'll have the exact cohort in a room, building. Let us find out."*
4. **Vercel** — *"Your DevRel role is chartered to track how developers build agents and where they get
   stuck. That's a study, not a job we can't fill fast enough. We can run it."*
5. **An AI lab (productivity)** — *"Nobody can credibly publish whether their own tool makes developers
   faster. A neutral party can. We can randomize a narrow AI-workflow condition and measure output."*
6. **Supabase/Neon** — *"You see your users, not the greenfield teams who evaluated you and chose
   someone else. We watch teams pick a stack from zero. Pilot on one cohort?"*
7. **Modern Treasury/Plaid** — *"You've said KYB latency kills deals but can't size it. We can observe
   where compliance stops an integration before launch."*
8. **Databricks/Snowflake (startup)** — *"Your startup team is measured on evaluation→production
   conversion. We can show you the counterfactual your own funnel can't."*
9. **DORA / a sponsored-research slot** — *"You already solicit sponsors. We can supply the neutral
   behavioral layer your survey can't reach."*
10. **Citadel** — the one where **sponsorship is the right first product** (proven intake, elite cohort),
    with research as an optional upsell — the easy first dollar while the research pilots run.

**The discipline:** each of these is a hypothesis with **UNKNOWN WTP**. The pre-sell test
([go-to-market.md](go-to-market.md)) is what turns any of them into a signed number — and the matcher
must be free to conclude, on a real conversation, that the fit isn't there.
