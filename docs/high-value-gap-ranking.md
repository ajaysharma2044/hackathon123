**Governing rule:** Evidence ≠ Claim ≠ Hypothesis ≠ Decision. Every dollar figure below is **real,
supply-side** evidence (a buyer spending, a market size, a filing) with a citation in the sweep
dossier. **Every "structural gap" is a HYPOTHESIS.** **No demand-side willingness-to-pay for our
environment is established anywhere** — that is the gating **UNKNOWN**. A rational value ceiling
(what winning is worth) is *not* observed WTP. Confidence tags: **KNOWN / LIKELY / UNKNOWN**.

Ranking is evidence-first, then decomposed (spend intensity · gap severity · hackathon advantage ·
buyer accessibility · Event-1 feasibility) — never a single collapsed score (`opportunity_matcher.rank`).

## TOP 5 economic battlegrounds

Chosen on the composite the message specifies — **money + urgency + scarcity + gap + hackathon fit +
accessible buyer** — evidence-first, and honestly de-ranking big wars where our fit is weak.

1. **Enterprise AI ROI / productivity attribution** (#1). Biggest *confirmed, buyer-tied* recurring
   spend with an **explicitly stated bottleneck** (Dimon on record). A neutral party has a structural
   advantage no vendor has. Fit is **MED–HIGH** (confidentiality + external validity are the real
   risks), not a slam dunk. This is the sharpest wedge.
2. **Parallel R&D search — drug discovery** (#2). Enormous cost base (Eroom), a genuine
   "independent-parallel-search" gap. Fit limited by domain-expertise depth; strongest as an
   *augmented* environment (domain experts embedded).
3. **Airline / logistics / manufacturing operations under shocks** (#3, #7, #14). Big buyer-tied
   dollars, and the **CRISIS_SIMULATION** archetype fits unusually well. Domain + data access are the
   constraints.
4. **Retail returns / reverse-logistics** (#4). The most surprising **non-technical** battleground:
   $890B, operations-shaped, good simulation/optimization fit, a clear ops buyer.
5. **Developer adoption & neutral choice data** (#6). The *old thesis*, now correctly demoted to one
   of five — highest natural hackathon fit, but historically **capped WTP** (sponsorship economics).
   Kept because fit + evidence are strong even if the premium price is doubtful.

> Deliberately **de-ranked**: the **AI-talent compensation war** (Meta $300M/4yr) is the loudest war
> by dollars, but our environment holds **low structural fit** for it (we are not a senior-researcher
> recruiter, and "measure which researchers move the frontier" is not hackathon-shaped). The message
> forbids forcing "elite builders" to be the answer — so we don't.

## Our best current candidate for the "AI-talent-war equivalent"

**What is scarce, and what we can uniquely supply:**

> **Neutral, independent, causal evidence — and parallel solution diversity — about whether an
> expensive bet actually works, on problems where no incumbent vendor can be trusted to grade its own
> homework.**

This is the scarce asset, **not** "elite student access." It recurs as the #1 structural gap across
almost every war in the sweep: buyers spend billions and **cannot buy proof it works** — AI ROI (JPM,
Dimon on record), credits→retention (AWS), media incrementality (P&G), AI value-creation (Blackstone),
breach reduction (cyber), AI drug hit-rate (Recursion). A hackathon-like environment run by a neutral
party can produce that evidence through *observed parallel experimentation* in a way a conflicted
vendor or a self-serving internal pilot structurally cannot ([structural-gap-map.md](structural-gap-map.md)).

**Chosen on evidence, not excitement:**

| Test | Reading |
|---|---|
| Money | **HIGH** — $2B/yr (JPM) inside sustained AI budgets; McKinsey 28% >10% ICT [KNOWN spend] |
| Urgency | **HIGH** — explicit executive statement the ROI is unquantified (Dimon) |
| Scarcity | **HIGH** — neutral causal evidence is genuinely unpurchasable today (no vendor can be neutral about itself) |
| Structural gap | **HIGH** — named repeatedly, and it is the reset's original Cluster B ("is AI actually making people faster") |
| Hackathon fit | **MED–HIGH** — the experimentation/parallel-arms archetype fits; confidentiality + external validity are real limits |
| **Willingness to pay** | **UNKNOWN** — the gating unknown; no buyer contacted |

**First wedge:** AI-on-work **productivity/ROI measurement** — biggest confirmed war + explicit stated
bottleneck + the one place a neutral party has a durable edge. **This does not assert WTP.** The next
step is unchanged and now doubly-sourced: build one polished sample deliverable and run the
falsification pre-sell against ~10 named buyers (CIO / AI-transformation / research-ops), trying to
close a **$5–15K paid pilot**. A signed check is the only evidence that moves WTP out of UNKNOWN.
