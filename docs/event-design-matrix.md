## How to read this

The features that **improve all four axes** (open track, 90-day follow-up, year-round panel offer,
elite mentors) are the no-brainers — fund them first. The **cash-vs-experience/longterm tradeoffs**
(continuation grants, extra sponsors) are genuine Pareto choices to make deliberately, not by
default. The **two hard boundaries** are non-negotiable regardless of the revenue they'd generate —
because a 10/10 builder experience and participant trust are core economic assets
([monetization-map.md](monetization-map.md)), not soft constraints.

Everything here is assumption-driven; the matrix is a decision scaffold, and each row's real numbers
resolve as evidence arrives ([quant-assumptions.md](quant-assumptions.md)).
