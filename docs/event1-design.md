# Event 1 Design & Final Synthesis

The concrete Event 1 recommendation, and honest answers to the 40 questions — grounded in the quant
engine ([event-optimizer.md](event-optimizer.md)), the value engines ([rd-engine.md](rd-engine.md),
[commercial-engines.md](commercial-engines.md)), the history ([historical-structural-analysis.md](historical-structural-analysis.md)),
and the epistemic discipline of [STATE.md](STATE.md). Every price is a ceiling; **WTP is UNKNOWN**;
the goal is the highest-value version we can actually sell and execute — not to prove the thesis.

## The recommended Event 1

**Do not commit expensive logistics until ≥1 paid pilot/LOI is signed** (the quant engine's dominant
conclusion; VOI says ~5–10 buyer conversations first). Conditional on that gate, the Pareto-informed
design:

| Variable | Recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Participants | **~150–200** curated builders | optimizer: 300 worsens cash+experience; 120 minimizes downside. 150–200 balances research power and cost |
| Duration | **72h** | enough for real integration-to-prototype; the research window (measurement.md) |
| Open / free-choice surface | **≥40%** unconstrained | protects the tool-choice baseline; below 25% with ≥4 sponsors triggers contamination |
| Corporate modules | **3–4 non-competing** | portfolio optimizer + capacity; one sponsor per competitive category |
| Engine mix | **1 Research (primary) + 1 Activation + 1 R&D/Innovation challenge + ≤1 Sponsorship floor** | the two spine engines + one directed challenge; sponsorship funds logistics |
| Research clients | **1 well-powered + 2–3 observational** | measurement.md: one primary study; the rest diagnostic |
| R&D challenges | **1** (only if a high-uncertainty, prototypeable problem is sold) | rd_fit gate — decline low-uncertainty |
| Mentor density | **~0.1** (elite), sponsor engineers disclosed | steep experience returns; also the help→capture channel |
| Research burden | **≤18 min/participant** | past ~25 the experience loss dominates the research gain |
| Follow-up | **7 / 30 / 90 days, incentivized** | the retention differentiator; never dropped |
| Travel/hotel | **funded via sponsor scholarships** | Reality Hack model; the largest cost line, offsettable |
| Continuation | **optional grants** | best long-term/experience, worst cash — a deliberate Pareto choice |

**Sponsors may NOT:** require exposure on every track (kills the baseline), see individual-level data
(aggregate-only except opt-in discoverability), get editorial control over conclusions, or run
directly-competing studies in one event. **Instrument:** brokered keys, artifacts, organic qualitative.
**Never measure:** keystrokes/screens/DMs, protected traits, any person score.
**Participants receive:** funded logistics, elite mentors, real freedom, a spotlight demo, opt-in
recruiting/VC/design-partner access, a continuation path. **Companies receive:** an aggregate findings
report (Research), an activation→90d retention funnel (Activation), N prototypes + a failure map (R&D),
all confidential per client.

## The 40 answers (evidence + uncertainty)

1. **What are we selling?** Primarily a **behavioral research instrument** on an elite builder cohort,
   secondarily **parallel R&D** and **activation** evidence — wrapped as a **directed wallet**. Not logos.
2. **Strongest commercial use?** **Research** (deepest proven budget) tied with **R&D** for uniqueness.
3. **Strongest R&D use?** Parallel search on **high-uncertainty, parallelizable, evaluable** problems
   + the value-of-failure map (rd-engine.md).
4. **Strongest product-dev use?** The **variation/convergence** distribution across 5–15 prototypes.
5. **Strongest research use?** "Why did the people who *didn't* choose you not choose you" — invisible
   to first-party telemetry (buyer-needs.md).
6. **Strongest activation use?** The **credit→retention** study (does startup-credit spend retain?).
7. **Historical structures that resemble this?** Research Instrument (LF/omnibus), Parallel R&D Search
   (InnoCentive/Topcoder/DARPA), Directed Wallet (SRC/Stanford HAI).
8. **Which failed and why?** Logistics-only hackathons (no attached value); builder communities that
   ran on novelty (On Deck, buildspace); IP-grab hackathons (backlash); mixing data+investment (Carta).
9. **Why us over an internal team?** We see the **non-customers and the counterfactual** they can't.
10. **Over McKinsey/IDEO?** Cheaper, and we produce **behavioral evidence + N independent prototypes**,
    not senior-consultant slideware — for the specific observable questions.
11. **Over Kaggle/InnoCentive?** We observe **building behavior**, not just score a submission, and we
    handle open-ended (not machine-scorable) questions.
12. **Over a university lab?** Faster, curated cohort, and a directed deliverable — but we **lose** to a
    lab on deep multi-month research; be honest.
13. **Over contractors?** We are the compressed, cheaper, evidence-rich version — for fit-able problems.
14. **Over ordinary sponsorship?** We deliver a **decision-changing answer**, not impressions — the
    only justification for pricing above the $5–50K commodity floor.
15. **Which problems benefit most from parallel teams?** High-uncertainty, parallelizable, evaluable,
    prototypeable-in-event (rd_fit).
16. **Which should NEVER come here?** Low-uncertainty, domain/equipment-heavy, long-cycle, or
    proprietary-context problems; median-population questions; enterprise-procurement decisions.
17. **How many teams before diminishing returns?** An **interior optimum** (optimal_teams) — larger for
    high uncertainty, small for low; *more is not better* (Boudreau/Lakhani).
18. **How should prizes be structured?** Enough to draw effort without distorting free choice; for R&D,
    size the purse to the marginal-value-of-the-next-team curve.
19. **How should IP work?** Buyer licenses/owns the challenge solution; participants keep everything
    else; **decided in the agreement before the event**, disclosed in the challenge.
20. **Follow-on development?** A promising team continues under a **paid design partnership** (no equity
    grab — venture-upside.md). The real upside.
21. **Which event variables matter most?** Research-sponsor count, free-choice share, scholarship
    coverage, 90-day follow-up, contract value (WTP).
22. **Which barely matter?** Oversized prize pools, venue prestige beyond technical needs.
23. **Which interactions matter?** free-choice × sponsor-count (contamination), builders × research
    quality × WTP (the flywheel), research-burden × experience.
24. **Next dollar of budget?** Protect free-choice surface + fund 90-day follow-up (improve all four
    objectives); then elite mentors and brokered-key instrumentation.
25. **Sponsors pay for vs get free?** Pay for research/R&D/activation deliverables + directed wallet +
    category exclusivity; free = logos and generic branding (the floor).
26. **Participants never subjected to?** Surveillance, person-scoring, feeling farmed, or a research
    cadence that taxes the build.
27. **Which company budgets can pay us?** Product/UX Research, Growth, Startup Programs, R&D, Innovation,
    Developer Marketing, Partnerships — the *same company* via different owners.
28. **ICP for Research?** Dev-facing company whose deciding behavior is invisible to telemetry.
29. **ICP for Product-Dev?** Product team exploring a solution space / feature demand.
30. **ICP for R&D?** Org with a high-uncertainty, parallelizable, prototypeable, evaluable problem.
31. **ICP for Activation?** Dev-facing company running credits/startup program needing behavioral adoption evidence.
32. **ICP for Sponsorship?** Any brand wanting exposure — the commodity floor, not the thesis.
33. **Which 10 to contact first?** [top-prospects.md](top-prospects.md) top-ten (credit-program owners,
    Stripe, Sentry, Vercel, an AI lab, Supabase/Neon, Modern Treasury, Databricks/Snowflake, DORA, Citadel).
34. **What to pitch each?** Their unknown, not our event ([go-to-market.md](go-to-market.md) theses).
35. **What falsifies the whole thesis?** 10–15 qualified buyers valuing it below $30K; a panel/telemetry
    seen as equivalent+cheaper; elite builders judged unrepresentative; procurement blocking multi-client
    event studies; no buyer pre-committing cash.
36. **Validate BEFORE Event 1?** WTP (the one gating unknown) — via ~5–10 buyer conversations / a paid pilot.
37. **Learnable only BY running Event 1?** Real retention curves, actual switching mechanisms, the true
    research-minutes tolerance, whether brokered-key instrumentation works at scale.
38. **What should Event 1 look like?** The table above.
39. **Max useful simultaneous engagements?** ~**3–4 non-competing** (capacity + contamination + one-per-
    competitive-category), not 8–12.
40. **Make the most money without hurting experience?** Load revenue on the **invisible/value-adding**
    side (brokered telemetry, scholarship-funded logistics, aggregate research, the directed wallet);
    the features that improve experience *and* revenue (open track, 90d follow-up, elite mentors) are
    the no-brainers; never trade the golden goose for one weekend's data.

## The single most important conclusion

Everything — the history, the archetypes, the value engines, and the probabilistic model —
converges on the same answer: **the highest-value, evidence-supported, sellable version of the thesis
is a Research + R&D instrument (wrapped in a directed wallet) for developer-facing companies whose
deciding behavior is invisible to their own telemetry — and the one thing standing between the repo
and a real business is a signed pilot that turns WTP from UNKNOWN into a number.** Build the sample
report, run the ten conversations, and let the first check decide the event.
