# Go-To-Market

How to reach firms — problem-first, never "sponsor our hackathon." Grounded in
[procurement.md](docs/procurement.md) (who signs, how they pay), [buyer-needs.md](buyer-needs.md)
(their real open questions), and the [engine VOI result](event-optimizer.md) (research ~5–10 buyer
conversations before committing).

## The message shape (from the archetype, not the event)

```
"We saw your team is trying to solve X   [their evidence — job post, launch, earnings]
 We're assembling Y builders             [the cohort, curated]
 The event naturally lets us run Z        [the engine — research / R&D / activation]
 which would answer/build/test A          [their decision, resolved]
 Would you be open to a paid pilot?"       [the ask — a check, not a sponsorship]
```

Never open with the hackathon. Open with **their unknown**. The [company matcher](company-opportunity-map.md)
generates this per company; [top-prospects.md](top-prospects.md) has the first ten.

## Who to actually contact (from procurement.md)

- **Not DevRel as the buyer** — their whole-year event budget is $40–200K and $100K exceeds it.
- **Best-fit buyers:** UX Research / Product Research (holds budget + a vendor path), Field/Regional
  Marketing (documented event discretion), Startup-Program owners (often in ventures/growth), Growth.
- **You need an internal champion** — you cannot self-register as a vendor. The first goal of every
  conversation is to find the person who *owns the decision and the budget line.*
- **Sell it as a pilot/sponsorship, not a services SOW** — pays before the event, lighter procurement
  (Atlassian's own paper splits ≥$20K into two invoices; 50/50 is standard).

## Timing

Q4 2026 corporate budgets are being set now; an anchor conversation after January 2027 sells into
allocated money. Startup-program and research budgets track the same calendar. **The window is the
next ~12 weeks** (roadmap.md).

## The sequence (what the quant engine says)

1. Build the **sample findings report** on one high-VOI question (the credit→retention or
   why-not-choose-us study) — the artifact that makes the pilot concrete.
2. Run **~5–10 buyer conversations** from the top-ten list. The VOI engine says this is
   positive-value; beyond ~10, more research is net-negative ([event-optimizer.md](event-optimizer.md)).
3. Ask for a **$5–15K paid pilot** (or a partner-event study — Wedge D) on the cheapest format that
   yields a real behavioral stream.
4. **Kill criterion:** no signed commitment / LOI in ~4 weeks means the owned event would have flopped
   too — resolve WTP before committing to expensive logistics.

## What a "yes" unlocks

A single signed pilot flips the WTP belief from **UNKNOWN → OBSERVED** ([beliefs.py](../engine/beliefs.py)
weights a signed deal ~4× a comparable), which moves the [event optimizer](event-optimizer.md) out of
the bear/base region and makes the owned Event 1 defensible. Until then, the honest position is: the
event is a bet on WTP, and the cheapest way to resolve it is these ten conversations.
