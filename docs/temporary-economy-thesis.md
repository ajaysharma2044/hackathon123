# The Temporary-Economy Thesis

> The top-level thesis after the reset. The prior "premium software hackathon → developer research →
> devtools buyer" model is **not discarded** — it is retained as **one labeled hypothesis** inside a
> much larger space (`environment.hackathon_flagship`, `business_model.SPONSORSHIP`/`RESEARCH_ENGAGEMENT`).

**Governing rule:** Evidence ≠ Claim ≠ Hypothesis ≠ Decision. **Decision committed: none.** Every
dollar figure cited here is real *supply-side* evidence; **demand-side willingness-to-pay for our
environment is UNKNOWN** — the gating unknown ([STATE.md](STATE.md)).

---

## The primitive

Not "a hackathon." The asset is:

> **A configurable temporary organization / market of high-agency talent, engineered around an
> expensive problem, whose behavior, choices, artifacts, and outcomes are observable, manipulable,
> and longitudinally trackable — run by a NEUTRAL party.**

A hackathon is one implementation. The engine ([discovery-engine.md](discovery-engine.md)) makes the
ICP, product, environment, talent mix, and business model **outputs**, not inputs.

## The 30-question synthesis

Answered from the engine + the seed evidence ([corporate-problem-universe.md](corporate-problem-universe.md),
[high-value-gap-ranking.md](high-value-gap-ranking.md)). "UNKNOWN" is used wherever evidence is absent.

1. **Fundamental asset** — neutral, causal evidence + parallel solution diversity about whether an
   expensive bet works, plus a longitudinal behavioral panel. Not "elite access."
2. **A hackathon, first-principles** — a short engineered temporary org that assembles talent +
   resources + incentives + feedback around problems and *observes what gets chosen and built*.
   Coding is incidental; parallel search under observation is the essence.
3. **Economic activities inside it** — labor allocation, resource markets (compute/mentor/capital),
   tool/approach choice, team formation, competition/collaboration, transactions (prizes/bounties/
   credits/introductions), artifact production, outcomes, and longitudinal follow-on.
4. **Missing from the old model** — non-cash shadow prices; non-research problems; non-software
   talent; non-hackathon environments; non-sponsorship business models; the spend×gap×fit demand
   lens; value-of-failure; demand-shaped event design. (All now in the engine.)
5. **Solved unusually well** — high parallelizability + prototypeability + experimentability +
   need-for-external-perspective, where a **neutral party has an edge no vendor has** and behavior is
   observable. Modes: DIVERGENCE, PROTOTYPING, SIMULATION, RED_TEAMING, FORECASTING.
6. **Should be killed** — high path-dependence / high political-friction / deep-domain-with-
   confidentiality / low-parallelizability convergence problems a single expert team does better, and
   anything a substitute does as well (the `problem_matcher` hard gate).
7. **Top 20 problem archetypes** — see [high-value-gap-ranking.md](high-value-gap-ranking.md).
8. **Industries that recur** — AI/cloud, banking/finance, insurance, retail, airlines, logistics,
   pharma, energy, media, PE, defense. The recurring *mechanic*: huge spend + no attributable read on
   whether it works.
9. **Buyers that recur** — CIO / AI-transformation office, R&D/innovation heads, COO/operations,
   product/growth, PE operating partners. **Not** primarily DevRel.
10. **ICP clusters (organic)** — (a) big-spend-can't-measure (AI ROI); (b) parallel-R&D-search
    (pharma/AI/defense); (c) ops-under-shocks (airline/logistics/mfg); (d) returns/reverse-logistics;
    (e) developer-adoption evidence (the old thesis).
11. **Strongest today** — cluster (a), enterprise-AI ROI/productivity: biggest *confirmed* spend +
    explicit stated bottleneck (Dimon on record) + neutral-party advantage. **WTP still UNKNOWN.**
12. **How weak is the evidence** — entirely supply-side; zero demand-side; some ⚠️ secondary sources;
    n≈200/elite/Hawthorne validity attackable; fit for several is MED not HIGH.
13. **Most surprising non-tech ICP** — **retail returns / reverse-logistics** ($890B) and **airline
    IROPS** — large, operations-shaped, good simulation fit, clear ops buyer.
14. **Format per top ICP** — AI-ROI → instrumented PRODUCT_LABORATORY + continuation; parallel R&D →
    OPEN_INNOVATION_CHALLENGE + domain experts; ops → CRISIS_SIMULATION; returns →
    OPERATIONS_WAR_ROOM; dev-adoption → classic hackathon.
15. **Is a traditional hackathon optimal for any?** Only developer-adoption evidence (native). For the
    rest, no — simulation/lab/tournament/continuation formats dominate.
16. **Resource flows with most value** — scarce mentor/domain-expert minutes; compute/data access;
    and the longitudinal follow-up (the compounding panel). Shadow prices **UNKNOWN**.
17. **Economic activity not yet captured** — non-cash shadow prices; value-of-failure (negative
    results); decisions-changed (the commercial numerator); longitudinal outcomes.
18. **Most informative behaviors** — unconstrained tool/approach choice, switching, abandonment,
    recovery, and 30/90-day continuation.
19. **Outputs companies would pay for** — neutral causal evidence; ranked prototype diversity;
    negative results (eliminated options); design-partner leads; opt-in work-evidence recruiting.
20. **Plausible six-figure contracts** — per-problem mandate, R&D contract, research engagement,
    research wallet, PE-portfolio contract. VOI *ceiling* high; **WTP UNKNOWN**.
21. **Better than consulting** — parallel independent search (breadth of approaches), speed, neutral
    causal evidence, behavioral realism, value-of-failure.
22. **Worse than consulting** — deep domain expertise, confidentiality, implementation, path-
    dependent/political problems, external validity of an elite student cohort.
23. **Better than crowdsourcing (Kaggle/Topcoder)** — interdisciplinary + behavioral observation +
    prototype diversity + in-person feedback + longitudinal follow-up. (Kaggle wins pure ML-metric.)
24. **Defensibility** — the compounding longitudinal panel + proprietary behavioral/outcome dataset
    across events + the neutral position + experiment-design methodology. **Not** the event (copyable);
    **not** passive intelligence alone (Orbit/Common Room were absorbed, not sustained).
25. **First validation experiment** — the falsification pre-sell: one polished sample deliverable on
    the sharpest wedge (AI productivity/ROI), 10 named buyers, open question first, try to close a
    **$5–15K paid pilot**; optionally layer the first real study onto someone else's event (Wedge D).
26. **Evidence to collect before choosing an ICP** — demand-side: buyer WTP signals, a signed pilot,
    which offer-shape (report vs access vs prototype) buyers pay for, and the panel-retention rate.
27. **What falsifies the thesis** — 10–15 qualified buyers value it <$30K; substitutes seen as
    equivalent/cheaper; buyers distrust elite/n≈200 evidence; procurement blocks multi-client; no
    pre-commit cash; the panel won't retain.
28. **If the devtools wedge isn't best** — replace with the strongest evidenced war+gap+fit: currently
    the neutral-causal-evidence / parallel-search family (AI-ROI measurement or parallel R&D), or
    ops-simulation. The engine chooses by evidence, not affinity.
29. **Optimal Event 1 under the new thesis** — **demand-shaped**: don't pre-build; sell one
    engagement first; its shape (talent/duration/format) falls out of the sold problem. If forced to
    pre-commit, the highest-fit default is a small, instrumented experimentation/simulation event with
    a continuation phase — **not** necessarily 200-person / 72h. (Note: Event 1 is now scoped
    **Cornell-only** — see [event1-cornell-design.md](event1-cornell-design.md) when built.)
30. **What the company becomes if it works** — a neutral **decision-intelligence + solution-search
    laboratory**: a repeatable machine that stands up configurable high-agency talent economies around
    expensive problems and sells the decision value / artifacts / evidence. The compounding dataset +
    methodology is the moat.

## The one thing that has not changed

Everything above is a **rational map of where value *could* be**. Not one line establishes that a
buyer **will pay us**. The single highest-value next action remains the cheap **falsification test**,
and the engine itself — run with WTP UNKNOWN — recommends exactly that (`bilevel.recommend_action` →
GATHER_EVIDENCE). Build nothing large until a signed check moves WTP out of UNKNOWN.
