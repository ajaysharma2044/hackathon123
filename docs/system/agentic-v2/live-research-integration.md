# Live Research OS integration

ResearchOpportunity connects a company question, economic importance, unansweredness, cohort and
event answerability, natural activity, capture and qualitative plans, artifact, buyer decision,
substitute, validity ceiling, participant value/burden, privacy and WTP status.

A configured opportunity_mapper receives accepted company claims. Valid proposals spawn unresearched
R&D/data nodes with their own completion contracts; invalid proposals are rejected in trace.
The mapper cannot mark them resolved. It must cite accepted factual/inferential claims.

The integration calls existing kernels:

- qualitative.assert_not_surveillance enforces the five capture modes and hard boundaries;
- CaptureStore/ConsentLedger preserve purpose-specific consent and revocation at query time;
- BurdenBudget is shared across opportunity plans, so each sponsor cannot reset participant attention;
- adaptive_questions.Tree constructs bounded “why” questions;
- adaptive_sampling.Sampler retains negative-case and burden-aware sampling;
- evidence_graph supplies finding/negative-case provenance;
- value_engines.RDProblem/rd_fit screens parallel search feasibility.

Explicit questions cannot be labeled zero burden. Negative burden debits are rejected by the legacy
kernel as well. Capture plans must be optional, must specify separate research/qualitative consent,
and require PRODUCT_TELEMETRY when applicable. Covert capture, keystrokes, screens, private messages,
individual scoring, forced product use and the existing inference boundaries remain forbidden.

This creates validated plans and reusable kernel objects. It does not deploy telemetry brokers,
collect participants, fabricate an interview, assume university approval, establish IP licenses,
or turn a small voluntary cohort into a population-representative or causal study. Sponsor-selected
exposure, self-selection, interviewer effects and generalizability limits must remain in the plan.
