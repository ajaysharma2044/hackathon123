### Why Decision Grid loses without being discarded

The generic Decision Grid is powerful as a **technical mechanism**: dollar-scored policies, robust optimization, hidden scenarios and asymmetric failure penalties. Its weakness is event architecture. Because it spans payments/routing/energy/operations, the data models and buyers fragment. A single sponsor's natural activity is less likely to fan out across a coherent product stack. GridCompute takes the best Decision Grid mechanic and places it inside one live economic battle with a deep recursive ecosystem.

## Winner falsification

| Attack | Result | What survives / fails |
|---|---|---|
| Cornell isn't special | **Partial hit** | MIT/Stanford/CMU can reproduce much of the talent; Cornell's advantage is intersection density and POWERWEB/ORIE/power lineage, not monopoly. |
| Students won't care | **Conditional survive** | “utility optimization” loses; “operate an AI factory under a hard power envelope” is much stronger. Must keep >=40% open-build freedom. |
| Buyer doesn't care | **Unresolved** | Economic problem is real; WTP for *this mechanism* remains PRIMARY. |
| Data isn't realistic | **Real risk** | Public/synthetic data works for a benchmark, not for production claims. Proprietary partner scenarios materially improve R&D value. |
| Can't solve in 48h | **Survives** | Teams solve bounded schedulers/controllers/policies against a simulator; nobody is asked to build a real data center. |
| Buyer already has substitute | **Partial hit** | Internal teams/consultants beat a hackathon for implementation. Event only wins on parallel solution search, failure discovery and varied architectures. |
| Outputs won't be trusted | **Real risk** | Sell a benchmark/prototype portfolio, not production validation. |
| Ecosystem is shallow | **Fails to kill** | Compute → scheduler/telemetry → power/cooling → grid/market layers create 3+ meaningful economic layers. |
| Revenue is generic branding | **Fails to kill if anchor sold** | The anchor deliverable can exist without logos: simulator/benchmark + solution/failure portfolio. Without that buyer, theme reverts to sponsorship and weakens. |
| Research becomes weird | **Controlled** | No hidden telemetry, no mandatory surveys, no keystrokes/screens/private comms, aggregate/opt-in only. |

## Kill criteria before Event 1 commitment

Kill or materially downscope the commercial thesis if any of these become true:

- 10–15 qualified buyers say the benchmark is not decision-relevant without proprietary production data **and** no partner will supply such scenarios.
- The closest substitutes (internal engineering, consultants, vendor labs, Kaggle/Topcoder-style challenge) are seen as equivalent or better at lower transaction cost.
- No buyer will sign a priced LOI or paid design-partner pilot before expensive venue/logistics commitments.
- Student testing shows the public framing feels like utility consulting/corporate homework rather than an ambitious AI-systems build.
