# GridCompute — Account Value Graphs and Sales Queue

## Account graph template

```text
Organization
├─ problem/scenario partner
├─ anchor R&D challenge / benchmark
├─ product integration + aggregate friction research
├─ compute / API / hardware / software support
├─ mentorship
├─ prizes (secondary)
├─ opt-in artifact-based recruiting
├─ paid continuation
└─ repeat annual benchmark / longitudinal program
```

Only natural branches are kept for a given organization.

## Six accounts to validate first

### 1. PJM Interconnection
- **Sourced current trigger:** PJM says nearly 4,000 MW of computational load unexpectedly disconnected in a July 22, 2026 event and is proposing new reliability requirements.
- **Problem:** large computational loads create planning/operations/reliability challenges.
- **Natural participant activity:** teams operate synthetic AI campuses through disturbance scenarios.
- **Potential deliverable:** diverse ride-through/control policies + failure/robustness map.
- **Likely decision affected:** technical requirements / scenario understanding / stakeholder solution search. **Needs buyer validation.**
- **Potential offer:** scenario partner or R&D benchmark partner.
- **Observed WTP:** UNKNOWN.
- **Fastest pilot:** ask whether a small public/synthetic benchmark would produce useful evidence; if yes, request one realistic anonymized scenario and a priced design-partner commitment.
- **Key objection:** student/synthetic outputs may not be reliable enough for operator decisions.

### 2. NVIDIA
- **Sourced connection:** current DCGM documentation exposes GPU power limits, group power budgets and workload power profiles.
- **Problem:** performance under finite power envelopes; operator usability of power-management controls.
- **Natural activity:** teams choose/configure power profiles and scheduling strategies.
- **Potential deliverables:** workload/power benchmark + integration friction + failure modes.
- **Likely owners:** data-center platform/DCGM/product/developer teams — **HYPOTHESIS**.
- **Observed WTP:** UNKNOWN.
- **In-kind potential:** GPU/compute access, technical mentors.
- **Conflict:** forced NVIDIA-only hardware would prevent neutral accelerator/product research; a dedicated optional track can avoid this.

### 3. Schneider Electric
- **Sourced connection:** 2026 controls/reference designs explicitly connect AI workload management with power, cooling and facility controls.
- **Problem:** integrated grid-to-chip/chip-to-chiller operation and deployment under finite power/cooling capacity.
- **Natural participant activity:** teams control facility digital-twin modules while scheduling AI work.
- **Potential deliverables:** alternative controls/optimization strategies, integration feedback, architecture portfolio.
- **Likely owner:** Secure Power / data-center systems / innovation — **HYPOTHESIS**.
- **Observed WTP:** UNKNOWN.
- **In-kind:** digital-twin/reference-design inputs, software, mentors, possibly equipment demo.

### 4. Microsoft
- **Sourced connection:** GridFM targets fast AC optimal-power-flow inference for volatile grids and specifically cites datacenter expansion.
- **Problem:** power-system scenario evaluation + AI infrastructure operation.
- **Natural activity:** teams use/compare grid models and compute policies.
- **Potential deliverables:** benchmark, model/application stress tests, research collaboration.
- **Likely owner:** Microsoft Research energy/grid + Azure infrastructure — partly sourced, buying authority UNKNOWN.
- **Observed WTP:** UNKNOWN.

### 5. DOE Office of Electricity
- **Sourced connection:** 2026 transmission needs study highlights data centers/large industrial loads; DOE is also studying oscillations from large data centers.
- **Role:** research/data/scenario partner rather than assumed sponsor.
- **Value:** public benchmark, education, solution diversity, reproducible artifacts.
- **Procurement/funding route:** UNKNOWN; do not treat as fast commercial revenue.

### 6. FERC
- **Sourced connection:** June 2026 show-cause actions demand large-load tariff reform.
- **Role:** policy/technical context source, possible judge/speaker/research stakeholder; **not assumed payer**.
- **Value:** see technical alternatives and student work; potentially improve public understanding.
- **Commercial status:** do not count in revenue forecast.

## Next 19 account research tasks

AWS, Google, Meta, CoreWeave, Oracle Cloud, Equinix, Digital Realty, QTS, Vantage, Vertiv, Eaton, Siemens, ABB, GE Vernova, Hitachi Energy, AMD, Broadcom, Arista and Dominion Energy each require a **specific-business-unit pass** before outreach:

1. identify current 2026 initiative;
2. identify actual operational decision;
3. identify current product/workflow and substitute;
4. identify decision owner and budget owner;
5. validate what event artifact could change;
6. find procurement route and closest comparable;
7. only then set a recommended ask.

No “Company X will pay $Y” statement is permitted before primary evidence.
