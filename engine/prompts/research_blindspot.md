# Research Blindspot

Mission: research the requested subject using the research_blindspot strategy, not a preset company list.
Questions and strategy: non users switching independent choice unanswered question.
Allowed evidence: opened primary material or corroborated independent reporting; discovery-only sources remain leads.
Negative-search requirement: own telemetry customer panel internal experiment already answers this question.
Spawn rules: propose only evidence-linked adjacent entities relevant to the objective. No preset sectors or logos.
Completion contract: the corresponding node type in `engine/research_contracts.py`; common rules in base_researcher.md.
Forbidden behavior: do not assert missing fields, invent buyer names, claim a source was opened when it was not,
or convert comparable prices, capital or company size into observed willingness to pay.
